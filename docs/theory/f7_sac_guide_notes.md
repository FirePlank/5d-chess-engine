# f7/f2 sacrifice guide (Lavamat, Steam guide 2218187793) — extracted notes

Notation: `*` = timecheck (softmate), `+` check, `>>x` time travel with capture. Lines are for
Standard (white travels to T1f7); in Turn Zero the same applies to both colours (T0f2 for Black).

## White's softmate lines after 1. Qxf7+ Kxf7 (on the new timeline)
- Qb3*: 2. Nh3 Nf6 3. Ng5+ Ke8/Kg8 4. c3 ... 5. Qb3*
- Qf3*: 2. Nh3 e6 3. e3 Nf6 4. Ng5+ Ke8/Kg8 5. Qf3*
- Qh5*: 2. Nh3 e6 3. e3 Qf6 4. Qh5*
- Qh5*: 2. Nh3 d5 3. e3 Bf5 4. Qh5*
- Other Black moves lose to one of these.

## White's travel routes to f7 (T1)
T5f3 and T5b3 (queen, the main threat), T7f1 (bishop), T6a2, T4f4, T4c4.

## Black's defences by opening
- Against anything: 1...Nf6 (forces the queen route via b3); 2...d5 then stops T1f7 access.
  If 2. Bb5 pins: contest b3 with a5-a4 or c-pawn pushes; main: 2...c6 3. c3 cxb5 4. Qb3 Qa5/Qb6/Qc7.
  2...Nd5 blocks but White answers Qh5 + bishop check, travelling to f7 on T3.
- Against 1. e3: 2. Bb5 a5 3. c3 a4 4. Qb3 axb3; 2...c5 3. c3 c4 4. Qb3 cxb3; 2...Nc6 3. c3 Na5 4. Qb3 Nxb3;
  2...c6 3. c3 Nd5 4. Qb3 Nxe3* (counterattack). Against 2. g4: 2...g5 3. c3 c6 4. Qb3 Qa5/Qb6/Qc7, or
  2...d5 3. g5 Ne4 4. Qf3 Be6.
- Against 1. c3: vs 2. Qa4 & 3. Qc4: 2...c6 3. Qc4 Qa5/Qb6/Qc7; vs 3. a3: 3...d5 4. Qb3 Qa5/Qb6/Qc7; 1...e6 2...f5 blocks f7.
- Against 1. e3 e6 / 1. c3 e6: contest f3 with Ne5; vs 2. Bb5, Qf6 to trade queens (piece down); 2. Qf6 forces the bishop to travel.
  vs 2. Qh5 (queen trade then T2 travel): don't trade queens immediately; wait until the softmate lines up.
- 1...e6 2...f5 blocks T1f7 (but Bb5+ and Bc4xe6* give White a mate in 4 after e3 opens).

## White's attack against moves other than 1...Nf6
Moving the f-pawn (f4 on T4/T5, f3 on T5) opens T7f1 -> T1f7 for the bishop; Black's other prevention ideas fail.

## Black's defences to each softmate
- vs Qb3*: open the c-pawn and play Qa5/Qb6/Qc7 on the attacking timeline; or a bishop on b4 on the original
  timeline to capture the queen arriving on b3. Jumping onto the diagonal from Qe7 is met by Ng5.
- vs Qh5*: h5 on the original timeline; White answers Ng5+ (delay). Kretreat -> Qh5*; Kxg5 -> Qf3*;
  Kg3 -> queen sac on h5 and knight softmate on h3. Black needs a pawn on h5 and Q/B on g5/h4/h6, and Nf6 on T1
  in the main timeline (else f3/f4 softmate on T8).

## Black's time-travel defences
Jump to create a timeline in the past, then capture the attacking queen two (sometimes three) turns up. Fails if
White plays Qh5* right after e3 or can disrupt the capture with a check; works if the capturing piece lands on a
protected square (Nf6 or the travelling queen) or g6 blocks the main timeline.

## King travel after Bxf7
Works only if White travelled with the bishop (not the queen) and Black opened with the d- or e-pawn on T1.

## Black's counterattacks
- T2f2 sac: f-pawn to f5 (T6/T7) or f6 (T7), travel from T8; Nf4+ keeps the f-file open. White defends with Nf3 up to T3.
- T1f2 mirrored sac: if White wasted move 1 (e.g. Nc3). White prevents it with Nf3 on T1/T2.
- Qh4 counterattack: mirror White's setup, queen to h4, sac on f2 (with a queen: instant mate). White: g3 (up to T3).

## White's king exile
Travel a knight to T2f5 on the attacking timeline (inactive softmate), then travel both kings and block past king
squares. Black: capture the knight from T1 (e.g. bishop T4c8 -> T1c5, then capture on f5). vs early queen travel
from T3h5, 1...e6 stops exile and softmates.

## Rules of thumb
- Black must open with Nf6 to use most defences.
- Black's preventions are safest; if they cost a piece without compensation, White just plays on the original timeline.
- White's best answer to defences is often to stop travelling and play on the original timeline.
- Don't trade queens immediately if White can exploit the open queen-less board.
- Set up defences as soon as the d-pawn moves (c6 then Qa5/Qb6/Qc7).
